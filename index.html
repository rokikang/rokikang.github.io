About Me

Hey there, I'm Roki — a student of Economics & Psychology, and an aspiring investor & psychotherapist. This is a personal site about ideas, thoughts, and books circling around my head.

I enjoy intuiting nuanced insights from complex human dynamics.

Hence, I'm also interested in:

  • Economics: Ecological Economics
  • Philosophy and Religion: Ethics, Moral & Political Philosophy, Buddhism (Madhyamaka, Zen, Engaged Buddhism)

...with hopes that learning about them will lead me, and hopefully our world, somewhere.
